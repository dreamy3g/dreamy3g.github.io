Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

English

1. Overview and Data Controller

The Daebak Korean (DAEBAK!, the "App") team provides a Korean grammar and expression learning service. No account registration is required. Learning progress and settings are stored mainly on the user's device.

2. Information We Process

  • On-device learning and settings: Grammar card views, practice progress, correct and incorrect attempts, completion dates, review cards, archives, onboarding status, cached content, and backup files the user exports or imports.
  • App identifiers: An app-generated device ID, user ID, purchase code, app version and build, locale, and creation or last-access timestamps. These support shared services, data association, support, security, and abuse prevention without account login.
  • Analytics: Firebase Analytics may process an app instance identifier, device model, operating system, approximate region, screen views, tab selections, card opens, practice starts and completions, review reveals, and similar interactions. The App is not configured to collect the Android Advertising ID and does not display third-party advertising.
  • Content reports: When a user chooses to submit a report, we receive the selected reason, content date, grammar item, related sentences or choices, an optional free-text note, user ID, device ID, and support code.
  • Purchase information: When a user purchases or restores paid features, the product ID, purchase status and time, store transaction, receipt or purchase token, RevenueCat anonymous customer ID, and entitlement status may be processed. We do not directly collect full payment-card or bank-account details.
  • Technical information: Firebase and RevenueCat may process IP address, request time, and app, device, or network information for service delivery and security.

3. Purposes

  • Provide and cache learning content and maintain local progress
  • Validate purchases, grant paid access, and restore purchases
  • Handle content reports and support requests and prevent abuse
  • Measure reliability and feature usage and improve service quality
  • Provide user-requested backup export and import

4. Retention, Deletion, and Your Rights

  • On-device information remains until the App or its data is deleted. Users control exported backup files at the location they choose.
  • App identifiers and content reports are retained while needed to provide the service or handle reports. We delete them when requested or when no longer needed, except for limited records required for security, disputes, abuse prevention, or law.
  • Purchase records are retained by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat under their policies for purchase validation, restoration, fraud prevention, and legal obligations.

Users may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of server-side information or to ask questions about processing. We may request a support code or similar information to verify and locate the relevant record. Deleting the App does not automatically delete store purchase records or all server-side records.

5. Service Providers and International Processing

  • Google Firebase — Analytics, Storage, Cloud Functions, and app infrastructure. Data may be transmitted over encrypted connections during App use and processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates. Retention follows service configuration and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. — Purchase validation, entitlement status, and restoration. Data may be transmitted over encrypted connections when the App starts or a purchase or restoration occurs and processed in the United States and other countries where RevenueCat operates. Customer records may be kept while needed to provide the service or as required by applicable law and policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — Each store independently processes payment and store-account purchase records under the Apple Privacy Policy or Google Privacy Policy.

6. Security

We apply reasonable safeguards such as encryption in transit, access restrictions, and data minimization. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 14. If we learn that information from a child under 14 was processed without valid parental consent, please contact us and we will investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when the App, law, or operational practices change. We will update the date above and provide notice in the App or on this page for material changes.
