2. Informational Use Only

Relationship-flow interpretations, response-flow insights, today\'s coach, and AI reports provided by the app are for reference only. They do not guarantee or determine the other person\'s actual intentions, feelings, or the outcome of any relationship.

AI reports and insights are generated using generative AI models and may contain inaccurate or unexpected expressions. Information provided by the app is for reference only, and final judgments and decisions about relationships are the user\'s own responsibility.

3. Ads and Credits

4. Data Storage and Backup

Signal Flow does not store user records in its own server-side account system. Users can back up their data directly through the export feature in the app, and we recommend backing up data before changing devices or deleting the app.
