Signal Flow Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Signal Flow stores some information that you enter or that is generated while you use the app in local storage on your device.

  • Daily entries: conversation count, reply time, initiated count, and mood score
  • Onboarding survey responses and daily survey responses
  • Daily analysis results such as today\'s coach, relationship flow interpretation, and response-flow insights
  • AI report results and related metadata
  • App settings such as theme, reminder time, and usage-tip status
  • Reward-credit status, starter bonus status, and device-identifier-based reward state

2. How We Use Information

  • To analyze and display relationship-flow patterns
  • To generate 3-day, 7-day, and 30-day AI reports
  • To schedule input reminders and maintain user settings
  • To support data backup and restore features
  • To review usage statistics for app quality improvement

3. Third-Party Services

The app may use the following external services.

  • Firebase Analytics: collects app usage statistics such as onboarding, entry saves, and report generation
  • Firebase Functions: processes AI report generation requests and reward-state checks
  • OpenAI API: generates AI reports based on your entries and survey responses
  • Google AdMob: provides ads, measures ad performance, and handles ad-related diagnostics and identifiers

4. Storage and Transmission

Entries, survey responses, daily analysis results, report results, and settings are primarily stored on your device. When generating an AI report, information needed for report generation, such as entry summaries, survey responses, and relationship type, may be sent to the OpenAI API through Firebase Functions. Some usage statistics may be sent to Firebase Analytics. For ad delivery and measurement, advertising identifiers or device-related information may be processed by AdMob and related third-party services.

5. Backup and Restore

You can use the export/import features in the app to back up or restore entries, surveys, daily analysis results, reports, and settings as an external JSON file. Signal Flow does not store your records in a server-side user account, so we recommend backing up your data before changing devices or deleting the app. Some reward-related data, such as ad credits, ad reward status, and starter bonus status, may not be included in backup/restore or may be handled separately based on device identifier information.

6. Your Choices

You can delete locally stored data at any time using the Reset All Data feature in the app. Input reminders can be turned on or off directly in Settings.

7. Ads and Reward Credits

Signal Flow may provide Google AdMob rewarded ads. If you choose to watch an ad, you may receive in-app AI credits that can be used to generate 3-day, 7-day, and 30-day AI reports.

  • Watching ads is optional and initiated by the user.
  • The ad reward structure and required credits may change as the service is operated.
  • AdMob and related partner policies may apply during ad delivery and measurement.
